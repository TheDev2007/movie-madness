All Time Favourites

  1. Inception - A mind-bending thriller that will leave you questioning reality.
  2. The Shawshank Redemption - A story of hope and friendship set in a prison.
  3. The Godfather - An epic tale of family, power, and betrayal in the world of organized crime.
  4. Parasite - A darkly comedic thriller that explores class struggle and social inequality.
  5. Interstellar - A visually stunning journey through space and time, exploring the depths of human emotion and sacrifice.
  6. Spirited Away - A beautifully animated film that takes you on a magical adventure in a world of spirits and gods.
  7. The Dark Knight - A gripping superhero film that delves into the complexities of good and evil.
  8. Forrest Grump - A heartwarming tale of an extraordinary man whose simple outlook on life inspires everyone he meets.
  9. Gladiator - A powerful epic about revenge, honor, and the fight for freedom in ancient Rome.
  10. Avatar - A groundbreaking visual spectacle that transports you to the lush world of Pandora, where humans and Na'vi clash in a battle for survival.
